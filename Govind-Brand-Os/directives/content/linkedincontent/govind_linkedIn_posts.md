## Post 1

Life after the AI bubble is going to be VERY interesting... 

Yes... we are in a bubble... but how big is it and what happens after it pops?

I talk about this in my latest YT video.

You can check that out here 👉 https://lnkd.in/gUWucG4U

TLDR; 

When it pops, the headlines will scream 

"THE MARKET IS CRASHING 😱 AI IS DEAD!!!" 

But the significance of the 'crash' will largely depend on the overall market demand for Applied AI. 

Here's the logic: 

1. The public market is HEAVILY weighted toward the AI giants like Nvidia, Google, Microsoft, Meta, and Oracle. 

2. The stock price for these companies is based on investor belief in future returns.

3. Nvidia has surpassed revenue expectations almost every quarter and remains the only 'real' GPU provider on the market. 

4. A slowdown does not seem eminent, however, the recent negative headlines around the lack of successful AI deployments might be hurting sentiment. 

5. "Will AI provide the amount of value to the economy that it promises?" is a question investors will start to ask more and more. 

6. If AI Integrators / AI Implementors / AI Systems Integrators / AI Automation Agencies / etc. are able to deploy AI systems within businesses that provide a statistically significant ROI (at scale), it's safe to assume that the overall demand for AI across the entire economy will increase rapidly. 

---

There will be a correction in the market. 

At some point, large investors will cash out, which will trigger a sell-off. 

The stock price for Nvidia et al. is astronomical due to:

1. The consolidation of talent and resources at the highest level
2. The insane amount of demand for AI products and compute
3. The massive economic implications of successful AI implementation at scale across the entire economy. 

The third point is the key... 

The effects of this inevitable correction will essentially come down to how fast AI companies like mine deploy high-ROI systems for businesses across the country (and world). 

If we can do this at scale, the market will correct and the bubble bursting will barely be felt. 

If we cannot do this at scale, many will seriously question the validity of AI and it's value to the economy - meaning the pop will be much more severe. 

We're ready to take on this challenge... are you?



## Post 2

I talked to dozens of VCs over the last 3-4 weeks... 

Here's what I learned 👇 

How VCs are thinking about the AI landscape: 
1️⃣ They're still running an outdated SaaS playbook. 

2️⃣ They're falling for what I call the 'Vertical AI Trap'.

3️⃣ There's a misunderstanding of where true value will be created when Agentic AI reaches full maturity.

4️⃣ They're still trying to identify moats within software, yet agree software will be instantly generated by LLMs via a prompt in the near future.

5️⃣ They agree that AI will catalyze a fundamental paradigm shift in the way businesses are operated, yet have no real framework for navigating the landscape.

Our Assumptions: 
6️⃣ Skills for white collar work will soon be made obsolete. 
- They don't fully align with the assumption that agents will be the primary users of software - they still see Agentic AI as a tool for humans, not an end-to-end operating system with intelligence, decision making, and tool calling abilities. 

7️⃣ It's the end of SaaS... 
- The ROI calculation behind deciding whether to build internal software vs buy off the shelf software is quickly shifting to the 'build internal' side.

8️⃣ Agent-builder platforms are old news
- It's time to develop the next layer of abstraction (natural language). 

9️⃣ Execution is free. Expertise isn't... 
- Our Agentic AI future will be more about data, insight, taste, strategy, etc. and less about software or the orchestration of tasks that require software. 

🔟 The Five Phases of the Paradigm Shift 
- We are currently at phase 2 and rapidly approaching phase 3.
