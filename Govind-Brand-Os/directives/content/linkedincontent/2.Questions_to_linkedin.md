# Questions to LinkedIn Post Generator

> **Purpose**: An interactive SOP where the AI agent asks targeted questions to gather content ideas, then transforms the answers into high-performing LinkedIn posts in Govind's brand voice.

---

## Goal

Engage in a structured Q&A session with the user to extract:
- Personal experiences and insights
- Business wins, losses, and lessons learned
- Frameworks and methodologies they use
- Opinions on industry trends
- Client success stories and case studies

Then transform these answers into LinkedIn posts that:
- Maintain Govind's authentic brand voice
- Follow proven LinkedIn post structures
- Simplify complex concepts for non-technical founders
- Drive engagement and conversions

## Process Flow

### Step 1: Discovery Questions

Ask the user the following questions in sequence. Wait for answers before proceeding to post creation.

#### Category A: Experience & Story Mining

1. **What's a recent win you had in your business that others might find valuable?**
   - What was the problem you solved?
   - What was the specific outcome/result?
   - What would have happened if you hadn't done this?

2. **What's a mistake you made that taught you something important?**
   - What did you do wrong?
   - What was the cost (time, money, stress)?
   - What did you learn from it?

3. **What's a contrarian opinion you hold that most people in your industry disagree with?**
   - Why do you believe this?
   - What evidence supports your view?
   - What's the common belief you're challenging?

#### Category B: Framework & Methodology Mining

4. **What's a process or system you've developed that gets consistent results?**
   - What problem does it solve?
   - What are the key steps/components?
   - What results have you or your clients achieved?

5. **What's something you do differently than others in your space?**
   - What's the conventional approach?
   - What's your approach?
   - Why does yours work better?

6. **What tool, technique, or strategy do you wish you knew earlier?**
   - When did you discover it?
   - How has it changed your work?
   - Why don't more people use it?

#### Category C: Client & Case Study Mining

7. **Tell me about a recent client success story.**
   - What was their situation before?
   - What did you do together?
   - What were the specific results (numbers if possible)?

8. **What's the most common problem you solve for clients?**
   - How do most people try to solve it?
   - Why does that approach fail?
   - What's your solution?

#### Category D: Trend & Opinion Mining

9. **What trend in your industry excites or concerns you?**
   - What are you seeing happen?
   - What do most people miss about this?
   - What should people do about it?

10. **What advice would you give to someone 2-3 years behind you?**
    - What do they focus on that doesn't matter?
    - What should they focus on instead?
    - What's the fastest path forward?

### Step 2: Evaluate Answers for Post Potential

After receiving answers, evaluate each for:

| Criteria | What to Look For |
|----------|-----------------|
| Specificity | Concrete numbers, real examples, named tools |
| Tension | Contrarian take, surprising insight, challenge to status quo |
| Transformation | Clear before → after story |
| Relatability | Problem ICP audience faces daily |
| Uniqueness | Fresh angle not seen everywhere |

**Select the strongest answer(s)** for post creation based on these criteria.

### Step 5: Format for LinkedIn

- **Short sentences**: 10-12 words max
- **White space**: Single line between thoughts
- **Visual markers**: →, ✓, ❌, 1️⃣, 2️⃣, 3️⃣
- **Bold headers**: Use 𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱 unicode for section breaks
- **Horizontal dividers**: --- between major sections

---

### Step 6: Optimize for Engagement

**Hook formula**: 
- Specific number/result + impossible claim
- Example: "This FREE AI system builds skills 10x faster than courses or coaching."

**Re-hook formula**:
- Parenthetical that deepens intrigue
- Example: "(And you can build it in 10 minutes)"

**CTA patterns**:
- Engagement: "Comment [KEYWORD] below"
- Connection: "1️⃣ Connect with me 2️⃣ Like + Comment"
- Scarcity: "Closing in 48 hours"

## Saving Generated Posts

**All generated LinkedIn posts must be saved locally** to maintain a content archive and enable version control.

### File Location
Save all posts to: `directives/content/linkedincontent/posts/`

### Naming Schema
Use sequential numbering starting from 1:
- Format: `{number}.{descriptive-title}.md`
- Examples:
  - `1.ai-skill-building-system.md`
  - `2.sales-call-training-framework.md`
  - `3.automation-roi-breakdown.md`

### Numbering Rules
1. **Check existing files** in the `posts/` folder before creating a new post
2. **Find the highest number** currently used (e.g., if `5.example.md` exists, the highest is 5)
3. **Increment by 1** for the new post (next would be `6.new-post.md`)
4. **Use descriptive titles** in kebab-case (lowercase with hyphens)
5. **Keep titles concise** - 3-5 words maximum

### File Content Structure
Each saved post file should contain:

```markdown
# [Post Title]

**Source**: [Question-based / User Interview]
**Created**: [Date]
**Status**: [Drafting / Review / Published]

---

## Hook
[First 2 lines of the post]

---

## Full Post

[Complete LinkedIn post content]

---

## Metadata
- **Content Type**: Text / Carousel / Image / Document
- **CTA Type**: Comment Below / DM Me / Like/Share / Visit Website
- **Target Audience**: [Specific ICP segment if applicable]
- **Key Topics**: [Tags/keywords]
- **Original Question**: [Which discovery question this came from]
```

### Execution Tool
Use `execution/content/save_linkedin_post.py` to handle file creation with proper numbering.

## Edge Cases

| Scenario | Handling |
|----------|----------|
| User gives vague answers | Ask follow-up questions for specifics |
| No concrete numbers | Use approximations or focus on qualitative transformation |
| Answer doesn't fit templates | Adapt the template or combine elements |
| Multiple strong answers | Create separate posts for each OR combine into one comprehensive post |
| User wants shorter post | Reduce to 300-400 words while maintaining structure |
| User provides transcript/notes | Extract the key insight and apply Q&A framework retrospectively |

**Agent asks**: "What's a recent win you had in your business?"
